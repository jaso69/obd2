1.9 TDI measuring blocks: which groups to check

A 1.9 TDI control unit gives its data in measuring blocks (groups): each group is a 3-digit number with four fields. Knowing which group to check saves a lot of time: many faults show up by comparing a specified value (what the control unit asks for) with the actual one (what it measures).

Distributor pump or unit injector

What each field means depends on the engine:

  • Distributor pump with a Bosch EDC15V control unit: the 90 and 110 hp 1.9 TDIs of the late 90s and early 2000s (for example ALH, AGR, AHF and ASV).
  • Unit injector (PD, “Pumpe-Düse”), with EDC15P or EDC16: later TDIs such as the 150 hp ARL. Instead of one pump there is a unit injector per cylinder.

OBD2Desk recognises the engine from the control unit identification and picks the right names itself. The names are for guidance and can vary between versions; the program works out the units with the formula the control unit itself sends.

Which groups to check for each symptom

Lack of power or limp mode

  • 011 · turbo: compare specified and actual boost pressure at full load. If the actual pressure does not reach the specified one, look for leaking hoses, the N75 valve or the turbo linkage; if it overshoots, the turbo may be sticking. The N75 duty cycle tells you how hard the control unit is trying to correct it.
  • 003 · air mass: if the actual air mass stays well below the specified one with the EGR closed (when accelerating), the MAF sensor may be faulty.
  • 008 · fuel quantity limitation: tells you whether the control unit is cutting fuel and why (engine speed, air mass or, on PD engines, smoke).

Black smoke

  • 003: too little air for the injected fuel. An EGR valve stuck open or a MAF sensor reading low causes smoke.
  • 011: if the turbo does not build boost, air is short as well.

Hesitation, rough idle or vibration

  • 013 · smooth running: the fuel correction for each cylinder at idle. One cylinder with a much larger deviation than the others points to that injector.
  • On PD engines, 018 and 023 show the status and switching of each unit injector.

Hard starting or injection noise

  • 004 · start of injection (distributor pump): compare specified and actual. If they are far apart, the pump timing may need setting, which is done in basic settings.
  • 001 and 007: coolant and fuel temperatures. A sender that reads too high or too low makes the control unit get cold-start injection wrong.

How to read them with OBD2Desk

  1. Connect with a KKL cable, ignition on or engine running.
  2. In Measured values, check the engine type under Engine and tick the groups you want.
  3. Click a row to see it in the live graph; with Ctrl+click you can compare up to four, for example specified and actual boost.
  4. Click Record CSV to save a test and analyse it later in the viewer. Road tests always with a passenger handling the laptop, and only where it is safe.

If something does not add up, the program's AI assistant can read these groups itself and tell you what to check.
